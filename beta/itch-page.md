# Coastline Rush — Android Beta

Offline arcade racing with nitro, shortcuts and a three-cup championship ladder.

Race through the coast, Neon City and Mountain Pass. Build your garage, customize your car and take on five rivals in a free Android beta.

## Choose your drive

- **Race:** Bot rivals, slipstreaming, boost strips, ramps and rough shortcuts.
- **Championships:** Five-, seven- and ten-race cups with increasing difficulty. Finish on the championship podium to unlock the next cup.
- **Time Trial:** Chase your personal best and race your saved ghost.
- **Career:** Endless traffic runs, coin collection and near-miss combos.

Earn game coins to buy and upgrade cars, change their paint and unlock weather worlds. Game coins are earned through play; this build has no real-money purchases or ads.

## Android download

Version **1.35**, Android **6.0+**, approximately **128 MB** download. Landscape play with tilt or touch-button steering. The game assets are included for offline play. Hardware performance varies; older phones may struggle with the detailed scenery.

Download the APK and open it on your Android phone. Follow Android's installation prompts for your browser or file manager. If installation is blocked, report the exact message and your Android version.

Existing testers should install over the previous version to keep local progress. Do not uninstall or clear app storage to update. Saves stay on this device; Android and browser saves are separate, and there is no cloud sync. Export/import backups are available in Driver Hub.

## New in 1.35

Graphics presets for lighter phones, adjustable tilt and corner assist, a guided first drive, smarter rivals, drift nitro, a mountain tunnel, milestones and save backups. Open Driver Hub from the startup page.

## Controls

Choose tilt or buttons on the startup screen. Hold the gas pedal to accelerate and the brake pedal to slow down. In races, double-tap gas to activate a full nitro charge. Hold your phone comfortably when enabling tilt; use recenter if needed.

## Help shape the game

This is an in-development beta. Please leave an honest rating and use the comments for feedback or bug reports. Include:

- Phone model, Android version and game version.
- Mode, route and controls you used.
- What happened, what you expected and how to reproduce it.
- A screenshot or short recording if helpful, with personal information removed.

We're especially interested in steering feel, pedal responsiveness, frame rate, audio volume and whether progress survives closing and reopening the app. Physical-phone compatibility testing is ongoing.
