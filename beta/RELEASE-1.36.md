# Coastline Rush 1.36 — Shortcut balance

Clean off-road shortcuts now offer a meaningful race advantage. The distance benefit eases in and out over the merges. Brief shortcut rail brushes have smaller penalties; prolonged scraping still slows the car. Main-road rail penalties are unchanged.

The entrance accepts cars safely positioned near the edge of narrower roads, without immediately pushing them against the shortcut rail. Both uneven crests, jumps and roadside obstacles remain.

Automated gate-to-merge comparisons cover 234 combinations across nine route/difficulty profiles, 3 km and 10 km courses, 100/200/280 km/h and 30/120 Hz steps. Clean runs save 0.60–3.13 seconds in these controlled comparisons, including approach and exit steering. Additional checks verify an actual brief rail contact remains worthwhile and equal strip boosts preserve the advantage. Real driving results depend on line and collisions.
